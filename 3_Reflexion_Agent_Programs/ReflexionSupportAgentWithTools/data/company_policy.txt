COMPANY CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY

1. Refund Policy
Customers can request a refund within 30 days of receiving their order.

A refund can only be approved when:
- The product is unused, or
- The product is defective or damaged.

Customers should provide their order ID when requesting a refund.

2. Shipping Policy
If an order is still being shipped, the customer should wait until the order is delivered before requesting a return.

If the shipment is significantly delayed, the support agent should check the order status before providing further instructions.

3. Damaged Products
If a customer receives a damaged or defective product, the company can provide a free replacement.

The customer may be asked to provide photographs or other evidence of the damaged product.

4. Return Policy
Returned products should be sent back in their original packaging whenever possible.

Returned products must normally be received before the refund is processed.

5. Customer Communication
Support agents must communicate politely, professionally, and clearly.

Agents should avoid technical or complicated language when communicating with customers.

6. Important Restrictions
Support agents must never promise a refund unless the customer's situation satisfies the refund policy.

Support agents must never invent company policies or provide information that is not supported by company documentation.

If information is unclear, the agent should explain what additional information is required.
